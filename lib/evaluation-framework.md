# Waleed AI — Evaluation Framework

Version 1.0 | Dreamy Partnership Assessment | 100 points total

## Scoring Guide

- **0–3**: No evidence or strongly negative signal
- **4–5**: Partial / weak signal
- **6–7**: Adequate — meets baseline expectations
- **8–9**: Strong — clear, validated signal
- **10**: Exceptional — best-in-class evidence

Score each sub-criterion on its defined scale. Sum sub-criteria to get the category total.
Write a one-line Rationale per category naming the specific evidence from the transcript.
If a sub-criterion has no data, score it 0 and note "No data."
Never fabricate traction metrics, market data, or customer counts not in the transcript.

---

## Category 1: Founder / Team — 25 pts

### 1.1 Domain Expertise (0–10)
- Has the founder worked in this industry for 2+ years?
- Can they name specific pain points that insiders would recognize?
- Do they know the key players, regulations, and dynamics in the MENA/Gulf market?

### 1.2 Execution History (0–10)
- Have they shipped a product, built a team, or closed customers before?
- Do they reference concrete outcomes (users, revenue, hires, contracts)?
- Is there evidence of learning from past failures?

### 1.3 Coachability / Dreamy Fit (0–5)
- Do they respond to pushback with curiosity or defensiveness?
- Are they open to Dreamy's 2-year co-founding model?
- Do they demonstrate self-awareness about their gaps?

---

## Category 2: Market — 20 pts

### 2.1 Market Size (0–8)
- Is the TAM clearly defined with a credible sizing methodology?
- Is the serviceable addressable market (SAM) realistic for MENA/Gulf?
- Are market size claims backed by data, not round numbers?

### 2.2 Market Timing (0–6)
- Why is now the right time? What has changed recently?
- Are there regulatory, technology, or behavioral tailwinds?
- Would this have worked 3 years ago? Why not?

### 2.3 Regional Dynamics (0–6)
- Does the founder understand Gulf-specific consumer behavior, regulation, or distribution?
- Are there KSA/UAE/Qatar regulatory considerations accounted for?
- Is the go-to-market strategy regionally grounded, not a copy-paste of a US/EU model?

---

## Category 3: Product — 20 pts

### 3.1 Problem Clarity (0–7)
- Can the founder describe the problem in one crisp sentence a non-expert would understand?
- Have they personally experienced the problem or done primary research with users?
- Is the problem acute (hair-on-fire) or nice-to-have?

### 3.2 Solution Differentiation (0–7)
- What makes this solution 10x better than the status quo?
- Does the differentiation rely on a defensible insight, not just features?
- Is there a clear unfair advantage (network, data, regulatory, distribution)?

### 3.3 Technical Feasibility (0–6)
- Can a 2-person Dreamy team co-build the MVP in 6–9 months?
- Are there major unknown technical dependencies or risks?
- Does the founder have a realistic sense of what needs to be built first?

---

## Category 4: Business Model — 15 pts

### 4.1 Revenue Model (0–6)
- Is the revenue model clear: SaaS, marketplace fee, transaction, subscription?
- Does it match how users already pay in this category in MENA?
- Are pricing assumptions grounded in market reality?

### 4.2 Unit Economics (0–5)
- Is there a credible path to positive unit economics?
- Do they understand CAC, LTV, and payback period conceptually?
- Are burn and runway assumptions realistic for the region?

### 4.3 Dreamy Equity Model Fit (0–4)
- Can Dreamy take equity instead of cash for its services?
- Is the founder comfortable with equity dilution at this stage?
- Is the company structure clean enough for equity participation?

---

## Category 5: Traction — 10 pts

### 5.1 Evidence of Demand (0–5)
- Letters of intent, pilots, paid customers, waitlist signups, or interviews?
- Is there any third-party validation of the problem or solution?
- Are these signals real (contracts, payments) or soft (expressions of interest)?

### 5.2 Momentum (0–5)
- Is the traction growing or stagnant?
- Has the founder shipped anything — even a landing page, prototype, or Notion doc?
- Is there evidence of customer pull vs. founder push?

---

## Category 6: Dreamy Fit — 10 pts

### 6.1 Co-founding Model Compatibility (0–5)
- Does the founder understand what Dreamy brings (tech + product, not just dev resources)?
- Is the 2-year co-founding timeline compatible with their plans?
- Is the founder ready to work closely with a technical co-founder from day one?

### 6.2 Bandwidth / Timing (0–5)
- Does Dreamy have capacity for this engagement now?
- Is this the right type of project for Dreamy's current team (mobile, web, AI, fintech)?
- Are there conflicts with existing portfolio companies?

---

## Scoring Interpretation

| Total Score | Recommendation |
|-------------|----------------|
| 80–100 | **Proceed** — Strong candidate. Move to PRD/SOW immediately. |
| 55–79 | **Conditional** — Significant potential with key gaps. Proceed with specified conditions. |
| 30–54 | **Watch** — Too early or too risky. Re-engage in 3–6 months if conditions change. |
| 0–29 | **Pass** — Insufficient evidence or fundamental mismatch with Dreamy model. |

If TOTAL < 30: print `WARNING: Sparse data — score below 30. Consider supplementing with a follow-up call before proceeding.`
