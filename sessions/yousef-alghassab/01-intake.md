## Product

**One-line description:** A healthcare review and recommendation platform that connects patients with doctors and facilities based on verified real-world experiences, with AI-powered review filtering.

**Problem being solved:**
- Patients struggle to find the right doctor or facility based on trustworthy, structured information
- Current approach relies on asking friends/family — no organised, credible source exists
- Existing reviews are often emotional, inaccurate, or unverifiable

**Current solution / status:** Concept / requirements definition stage — no MVP yet

**Key features mentioned:**
- Search by specialty, facility, or location
- Doctor profiles coded (not always named publicly) to reduce defamation risk
- Patient can request doctor's name privately through the app
- Review submission post-visit with smart AI filtering for objectivity
- Post-visit reminder system to capture reviews at the right moment
- Verification layer: appointment linkage, location data, delivery service integration
- Provider dashboard with analytics (demand by specialty, review counts, geographic expansion data)
- Provider can publish educational content (articles, videos)
- Admin panel: full content moderation, doctor/facility verification, document management

**Technical approach:**
- AI used for: review filtering, text analysis, natural-language data collection (conversational/voice-call style rather than rigid survey)
- Scientific methodology for doctor scoring discussed (not purely subjective)
- Platform scope: mobile app (implied); web not explicitly discussed
