## English Proposal Letter

Dear Yousef,

Thank you for the time you've given us across our two meetings — the requirements session and the proposal discussion. We've completed our internal evaluation of أمين, and we're pleased to formally extend this co-founding partnership proposal.

### What Dreamy Offers

Dreamy is a technical co-founding studio based in the Gulf. We partner with early-stage founders in MENA to build their products from zero to launch — and stay embedded for two years to see them through. We are not a dev shop. We take equity because we're betting on the project alongside you.

For أمين, we propose to deliver:

1. Full UI/UX design for all user flows (patient, provider, admin) — Arabic RTL-first
2. Coded doctor profile system with private in-app name request flow
3. Review submission with multi-signal verification (appointment, location, manual)
4. AI-powered review filtering for content quality and objectivity
5. Post-visit reminder system (automated push notifications)
6. Provider analytics dashboard
7. Admin moderation and provider verification panel
8. Full launch support through beta testing and v1 public release

### Proposed Timeline

- **Week 4:** Design complete, architecture signed off
- **Month 3:** Core patient flows live in staging
- **Month 6:** Full MVP feature-complete
- **Month 9:** v1 public launch on iOS, Android, and web

### Equity Terms

In exchange for our co-founding services, Dreamy proposes:

- **Equity stake:** 30% of أمين (new entity)
- **Vesting:** Progressive over the 2-year co-founding term, milestone-linked
- **Cash compensation:** None — pure equity engagement
- **Board representation:** Observer seat for the co-founding term

### Why Dreamy

What stood out in your approach to أمين is that you've already solved the hardest design problem before writing a line of code: how to make healthcare reviews trustworthy and legally defensible in the Saudi context. The coded doctor profile system is a genuinely sophisticated insight. Dreamy brings the technical depth to implement that system correctly — and the product experience to make it invisible to end users. The combination of your domain intuition and Dreamy's build capability is the right founding team for this product.

### Next Steps

We'd welcome your response to this proposal, along with any questions you'd like to work through. A short call to align on the path forward would be our suggested next step.

We look forward to building أمين together.

Warm regards,

Waleed Al-Sanosi
Product Manager, Dreamy
dreamybuilders.com
