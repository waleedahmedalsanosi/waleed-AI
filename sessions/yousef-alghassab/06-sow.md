# Scope of Work

**Company:** أمين (Amin)
**Engagement Type:** Technical Co-founding Partnership
**SOW Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-06-26
**Parties:** Yousef Al-Ghassab, أمين ("the Startup") and Dreamy ("the Co-founder")

---

## 1. Engagement Overview

Dreamy will serve as the technical co-founding partner for أمين, providing product strategy, design, and engineering services in exchange for an equity stake in the company. This engagement covers the development of the v1 MVP as defined in the Product Requirements Document dated 2026-06-26.

Dreamy's role is not that of a contracted development agency. Dreamy embeds as a founding technical partner — contributing to product direction, technical architecture, and go-to-market execution alongside the founding team for the duration of the co-founding term.

---

## 2. Dreamy's Deliverables

| # | Deliverable | Description | Target Date |
|---|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Design & architecture | Complete UI/UX design (all user flows), technical architecture documentation, database schema, API contracts | Week 4 from start |
| 2 | Core patient flows | Patient registration, search and browse by specialty/location, provider result pages | Month 3 |
| 3 | Coded doctor profile system | Doctor code management system, in-app private name request flow, admin code assignment tool | Month 3 |
| 4 | Review submission & verification | Post-visit review form, verification layer (geolocation + appointment linkage + manual), AI review filtering integration | Month 6 |
| 5 | Post-visit reminder system | Automated push notification trigger post-confirmed-visit with deep link to review form | Month 6 |
| 6 | Provider dashboard | Provider profile creation, analytics dashboard (review counts, specialty demand, trends), review response functionality | Month 5 |
| 7 | Admin moderation panel | Review moderation queue, provider onboarding queue, doctor code management, document verification interface | Month 5 |
| 8 | Authentication & onboarding | Patient OTP-based registration, provider document-upload registration flow, admin login | Month 2 |
| 9 | Beta testing support | Support for 3–5 pilot facility onboarding, bug resolution during beta period, performance testing | Month 7 |
| 10 | v1 launch | Production deployment, monitoring setup, launch-ready product | Month 9 |

**Deliverable quality standard:** Each deliverable is considered complete when it meets the acceptance criteria defined in the PRD v1.0, passes internal QA testing, and has been tested with a minimum of 5 real users at each beta milestone.

---

## 3. Startup's Responsibilities

The Startup commits to:

1. **Product decisions:** Final product decisions rest with the Startup founder. Dreamy provides recommendations; the founder approves all major scope or design decisions.
2. **Domain expertise access:** Providing Dreamy team access to healthcare provider contacts, market knowledge, and regulatory guidance required to build the product correctly — including facilitating introductions to at least 3 pilot facilities by Month 4.
3. **Feedback cadence:** Weekly 30-minute sync with Dreamy's lead during active build phases; response to design feedback within 48 hours to avoid blocking progress.
4. **User testing:** Recruiting a minimum of 20 beta patient-users and 3 pilot healthcare facilities for testing at Month 7.
5. **Legal & compliance:** Ensuring the company is properly incorporated before development begins; equity transfer is legally executable in the relevant jurisdiction; Saudi PDPL compliance obligations are understood and owned by the Startup.
6. **Branding assets:** Providing logo, colour palette, and any existing brand guidelines to Dreamy by Week 2 of the engagement — or confirming that Dreamy should design the brand identity as an additional deliverable.
7. **Government platform:** Resolving the government platform registration and renewal cost structure before contract signing.

## 5. Equity Terms

**Dreamy's equity stake:** 30% (proposed by Waleed Al-Sanosi; subject to Samir Al-Rashidi's review and confirmation)

**Vesting schedule:** Progressive vesting over 2-year co-founding term; Dreamy receives equity tranches at defined milestones aligned with development progress and partnership term.

**Anti-dilution:** Pro-rata rights in any future funding round up to Series A.

**Board representation:** Observer seat on the board of directors for the duration of the co-founding term.

*Note: Final equity percentage and vesting structure are subject to Samir Al-Rashidi's review and approval, and will be confirmed in the formal partnership agreement. The 30% figure is an indicative proposal.*

## 6. Intellectual Property

- All code, designs, and intellectual property produced under this engagement vest in أمين upon equity agreement execution.
- Dreamy retains the right to reference this engagement as a portfolio case study (with Yousef Al-Ghassab's written approval on specific content).
- Any pre-existing IP brought by Dreamy (reusable components, frameworks, internal tools) remains Dreamy's property; all derivative works and product-specific implementations belong to the Startup.

---

## 7. Exclusions

The following are explicitly out of scope for this SOW:

1. Direct appointment booking integration — deferred to Phase 2
2. In-platform recruitment / job listings — future feature
3. Advanced paid subscription tier for providers — post-launch
4. Cross-border GCC expansion — v1 Saudi Arabia only
5. Public doctor naming (as opposed to coded profiles) — pending legal validation
6. Delivery service / transport integration — deferred
7. Cash payment to Dreamy — equity-only engagement
8. Ongoing maintenance post the 2-year co-founding term — subject to separate renewal agreement

---

## 8. Assumptions

This SOW is based on the following assumptions. Material changes require SOW revision:

1. The Startup is (or will be) incorporated as a new legal entity before development begins — Dreamy and the Startup agree that the partnership will be entered into via a new company, not the founder's existing entity.
2. Yousef Al-Ghassab and his co-founder are the primary decision-makers with authority to grant equity in the new entity.
3. The co-founder's identity and equity position will be disclosed and agreed upon before contract signing.
4. Saudi PDPL compliance is the Startup's legal responsibility; Dreamy will build the technical infrastructure to support compliance but will not provide legal advice.
5. A minimum of 3 healthcare facility partners will be identified by the Startup before or during the beta phase to ensure meaningful user testing.
