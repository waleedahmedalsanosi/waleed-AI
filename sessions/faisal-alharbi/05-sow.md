# Scope of Work

**Company:** Munaqis (مناقص)
**Engagement Type:** Technical Co-founding Partnership
**SOW Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-06-26
**Parties:** Faisal Al-Harbi, Munaqis ("the Startup") and Dreamy ("the Co-founder")

---

## 1. Engagement Overview

Dreamy will serve as the technical co-founding partner for Munaqis, providing product strategy, design, and engineering services in exchange for an equity stake in the company. This engagement covers the development of the v1 MVP as defined in the Product Requirements Document dated 2026-06-26.

Dreamy's team will be embedded with the Startup for the duration of the 2-year co-founding term, acting as the full engineering and product function. The founder brings domain expertise, customer relationships, and market access; Dreamy brings everything required to build and ship the product.

---

The following deliverables will be produced by Dreamy within the engagement period:

| # | Deliverable | Description | Target Date |
|---|-------------|-------------|-------------|

## 3. Startup's Responsibilities

The Startup commits to:

1. **Company incorporation:** Register a Saudi legal entity (LLC or equivalent) before development begins. Equity grant is legally unenforceable without a registered entity — this is a non-negotiable prerequisite.
2. **Payment gateway setup:** Obtain a merchant account with Moyasar (or agreed alternative) in the Startup's legal entity name before billing integration begins (engagement week 18).
3. **Product decisions:** Final product decisions rest with the founder. Dreamy provides recommendations; the founder approves all design and scope changes.
4. **Domain expertise access:** Provide Dreamy team access to sample qualification documents, real tender checklists (3–5 examples), and introductions to pilot contractors as needed.
5. **Feedback cadence:** Attend weekly 30-minute sync with Dreamy's lead throughout the build phases, and be responsive on WhatsApp/Slack within 24 hours for unblocking decisions.
6. **Beta user recruitment:** Source a minimum of 5 contractor contacts willing to test the platform before launch; coordinate scheduling for beta sessions.
7. **API investigation:** Pursue official API access requests with MoF/Etimad contacts using founder's government network. Share findings with Dreamy by end of week 1.

## 6. Intellectual Property

- All code, designs, and intellectual property produced under this engagement vest in Munaqis upon equity agreement execution.
- IP transfers upon equity execution — not upon payment, as there is no cash component.
- Dreamy retains the right to reference this engagement as a portfolio case study (with founder's prior written approval on specific content before publication).
- Any pre-existing Dreamy IP (component libraries, deployment infrastructure, reusable frameworks) remains Dreamy's property. Derivative works built on that foundation for Munaqis belong to the Startup.

---

## 7. Exclusions

The following are explicitly out of scope for this SOW:

1. Government portal API integration (Etimad, Monafasat) — deferred to Phase 2 pending API feasibility confirmation
2. AI-based tender recommendation engine — deferred to Phase 2
3. Mobile applications (iOS / Android) — web-first in v1; mobile deferred
4. Gulf market expansion (Kuwait, UAE) — deferred post-KSA product-market fit
5. Multi-user / team accounts within a single company — deferred to Phase 2
6. Automated tender discovery and portal monitoring — deferred pending API policy clarity
7. Cash payment to Dreamy — this is an equity-only engagement; Dreamy receives no cash fees
8. Ongoing maintenance beyond the 2-year co-founding term — subject to a separate renewal agreement

---

## 8. Assumptions

This SOW is based on the following assumptions. If they change, the SOW must be revised and re-signed:

1. Munaqis is (or will be) incorporated as a Saudi legal entity before development begins.
2. Faisal Al-Harbi is the primary decision-maker with legal authority to grant equity to Dreamy.
3. Government portal API access is not assumed in v1 scope — the product is designed to work as a manual-input, manual-submission tool. If API access becomes available, portal integration will be scoped as a SOW amendment.
4. The build is executed by a 2-person Dreamy team. Timeline adjustments will be required if team composition changes.
5. The founder will be available for a weekly 30-minute sync throughout the engagement.
