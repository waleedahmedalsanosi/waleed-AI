## English Proposal Letter

Dear Faisal,

Thank you for sharing your vision for Munaqis during our recent meeting. Your background — seven years running procurement at Saudi Aramco, then manually helping contractors prepare their qualification files at Riyad Consulting — is exactly the kind of founder-market fit that makes us take notice. You didn't find this problem through research; you charged clients to solve it by hand. That changes the nature of the opportunity.

We've completed our internal evaluation, and we're pleased to extend this partnership proposal.

### What Dreamy Offers

Dreamy is a technical co-founding studio for MENA/Gulf founders. We partner with domain experts who know their market deeply but need a technical partner to build and ship the product. We don't operate as a dev shop — we embed as co-founders, take equity, and stay for the long run.

For Munaqis, we propose to deliver:

1. **Technical Discovery** — API feasibility assessment for Etimad and Monafasat; architecture finalized; stack decided
2. **Full Product Design** — every screen designed in Figma with Arabic RTL UI from day one, approved by you before a line of code is written
3. **Contractor Profile & Document Vault** — the data foundation: company record, certifications, license expiry tracking, searchable project history
4. **Qualification File Generator** — the core value: enter a tender's requirements, generate a professional PDF packet from your stored data in minutes instead of hours
5. **Tender Deadline Tracker** — calendar view with automated email and SMS reminders so no deadline slips through
6. **Subscription & Billing** — two-tier plans (SAR 500 and SAR 1,500/month), local payment gateway (Moyasar), VAT-compliant tax invoices
7. **v1 Launch** — production deployment with your first paying contractors onboarded, within approximately 7 months of engagement start

### Proposed Timeline

| Phase | Target |
|-------|--------|
| Discovery & Design | Weeks 1–4 |
| Core features build | Weeks 5–12 |
| MVP feature-complete | Week 22 |
| Beta testing | Weeks 23–26 |
| v1 public launch | Week 28 (~7 months) |

### Equity Terms

In exchange for our co-founding services, Dreamy proposes:

- **Equity stake:** [___]% of Munaqis

### Why This Works

You have the rarest combination in B2B SaaS: a problem you lived from the inside, customers you already know, and a clear product insight — enter data once, generate for any tender. Dreamy has built web SaaS platforms with Arabic RTL, Saudi payment integrations, and Gulf-market compliance before. This is a natural fit on both sides.

The one structural item to resolve before we can begin: Munaqis needs to be a registered Saudi legal entity. Without incorporation, equity cannot be issued, and the partnership agreement cannot be executed. If that's in motion, we can move quickly.
