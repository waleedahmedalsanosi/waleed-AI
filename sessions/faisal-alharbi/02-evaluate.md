## Key Strengths

1. **Domain expertise is real and verifiable.** 10 years in procurement, personally charged clients to manually solve this exact problem at Riyad Consulting. This is the most credible founder-market fit signal available.
2. **40+ warm leads from prior consulting relationships.** First-customer acquisition risk is lower than typical cold-start SaaS. Faisal can call 10 contractors tomorrow and have pilots.
3. **Phase 1 works without API integration.** Even without Etimad/Monafasat API access, organizing contractor profiles and streamlining document preparation is a real product. The API-dependent auto-generation is a Phase 2 enhancement, not a prerequisite.

## Key Risks

1. **Company not incorporated.** Equity issuance is legally impossible until a Saudi entity exists. This is not a formality — it is a legal blocker that must close before Dreamy commits resources.
2. **Government portal API access unconfirmed.** The auto-generation pitch depends on Etimad and Monafasat allowing API reads. Saudi government portals have historically been hostile to third-party integrations. If APIs are unavailable, the product's flagship feature becomes manual and the differentiation narrows.
3. **Solo founder, no team.** Faisal has zero technical capability. Dreamy carries 100% of product and engineering execution. If the engagement hits friction, there is no redundancy. This increases execution risk for Dreamy.

## Conditions

1. **Incorporate the company** (Saudi LLC or appropriate structure) before Dreamy signs any agreement. Equity must be legally executable.
2. **Confirm API feasibility** — conduct technical discovery with Etimad/Monafasat before finalizing product scope. If APIs are unavailable, revise the v1 feature set to a manual-input model and reset the founder's expectations accordingly.
3. **Negotiate equity** to Dreamy's standard co-founding range. 20% is a starting position; confirm Dreamy's floor with Samir before the next founder conversation.
4. **Secure at least one LOI or paid pilot commitment** from the 9 interested contractors before Dreamy begins build. This converts verbal interest to commercial signal.
